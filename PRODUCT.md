## Platform

## Stack
Delegated by user request to proceed in one pass. Static HTML/CSS/ES modules, Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Storage; prepared for Netlify.
## Users
Visitors browse exhibitions without login. Owner and approved staff manage exhibitions via Google sign-in.
## Product Purpose
Exhibition discovery, detail reading and practical staff publishing in one integrated site: home at /, exhibition list at /exhibition, details at /exhibition/:id and admin at /admin.
## Capabilities and Constraints

Supplied homepage design and business footer content are retained. Homepage and exhibition list share a live published exhibition source. Other legacy pages and policy pages remain pending migration. No production credentials, domain access or actual exhibition assets were supplied.
